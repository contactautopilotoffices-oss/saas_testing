What we do

One partner across the entire workspace lifecycle

Most companies stitch together a broker, a designer, a contractor and a facility vendor, then spend the next three years managing all four. Autopilot holds the whole chain, so accountability never sits in a gap between suppliers.

Strategy & Sourcing

We start with the business case, not the floor plan. Headcount plans, locations your people can actually reach, commercial terms worth signing. We shortlist, negotiate and take the space to the point where a decision is easy to defend internally.

Design & Build

Design that reflects how your teams work, delivered under a single schedule and a single budget line. One team owns drawings, approvals, vendors, site quality and handover dates.

Ready-to-Move-In

Fully fitted, serviced offices your team can occupy without a capex cycle. The space, the furniture, the connectivity and the daily operations arrive together.

Integrated Facility Management

The part that decides how the workspace actually feels. Housekeeping, technical upkeep, pantry, security coordination, vendor management and reporting, run to a defined standard and measured against it.

The Day 1 Standard

The office is not ready when the keys arrive.
It is ready when nobody has to ask for anything.

  • Every seat, screen and meeting room working before the first person sits down
  • Network, access control and support desk live from the first morning
  • Housekeeping, pantry and upkeep running to a written standard, not to memory
  • A named Autopilot team on site, accountable for the space from day one
  • Snags closed before handover, not tracked for months afterwards

That is what we mean by invisible excellence. The work is visible to us. The result is quiet for you.

ATLAS

The operating system behind every Autopilot workspace

ATLAS is the platform our teams run on. Every request, asset, inspection, vendor and service schedule across your workspace sits in one place, so a question about your office has an answer rather than an investigation.

  • A single record of every asset and every service across sites
  • Requests, approvals and escalations tracked end to end
  • Preventive schedules that run on time, not on reminders
  • Reporting your facility and finance teams can use directly
