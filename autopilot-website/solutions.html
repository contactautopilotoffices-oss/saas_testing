Solutions

One partner across the entire workspace lifecycle

Four ways to work with us. Most clients start with one and grow into the rest, which is the point: the team that sourced the space is the team that still runs it three years later.

Strategy & Sourcing

We start with the business case, not the floor plan. Headcount plans, locations your people can actually reach, commercial terms worth signing. We shortlist, negotiate and take the space to the point where a decision is easy to defend internally.

Design & Build

Design that reflects how your teams work, delivered under a single schedule and a single budget line. One team owns drawings, approvals, vendors, site quality and handover dates.

Ready-to-Move-In

Fully fitted, serviced offices your team can occupy without a capex cycle. The space, the furniture, the connectivity and the daily operations arrive together.

Integrated Facility Management

The part that decides how the workspace actually feels. Housekeeping, technical upkeep, pantry, security coordination, vendor management and reporting, run to a defined standard and measured against it.

In detail

What each one covers

Custom-Built Office

A workspace designed around your business rather than around a landlord's existing fit-out. We source the building, design to your ways of working, build it and then run it.

Ready-to-Move-In

For teams that need to be operational now. Fitted and serviced space, with the operations already running on the day you arrive.

Design & Build

Where you already hold the space. We take the brief, the drawings, the approvals, the contractors and the handover, under one accountable team.

Integrated Facility Management

The daily running of the workspace, to a written standard, reported against it. Housekeeping, technical, pantry, security coordination and vendor management.
